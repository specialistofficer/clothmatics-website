Privacy summary

ClothMatics uses your information to operate your account, maintain your wardrobe, show saved outfits and plans, provide personal app features, answer support requests, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information or use garment information for advertising.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the ClothMatics mobile app, website, Help and Contact page, and related services. It does not apply to independent websites or services you choose to visit from an external link.

3. How we use information

  • Sign you in and keep your account available across the app and website.
  • Create, organize, search, and display your digital wardrobe.
  • Show saved outfits, plans, wear history, cost-per-wear insights, preferences, and private Closet Quest progress.
  • Provide personal outfit and garment features in the mobile app.
  • Answer support, privacy, grievance, and account-deletion requests.
  • Maintain service quality, enforce reasonable usage limits, and prevent misuse.

4. AI-supported features

The mobile app may use AI-supported features for garment understanding, style feedback, and outfit suggestions. These features may use the photo, garment details, preferences, and occasion information needed to provide the requested result.

The ordinary user website does not start AI styling or garment analysis. Smart Purchase compares a possible purchase with wardrobe information already available to you, and its optional photo is not added to your closet.

AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate. Treat styling results as suggestions and review them before making a decision.

5. Service providers and disclosure

We use trusted service providers for sign-in, data storage, website delivery, AI-supported app features, email, and service monitoring. They may process information only as needed to provide those services and under their own privacy commitments.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or during a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Account, wardrobe, profile, outfit, planner, wear, and private Closet Quest information is generally kept while your account remains active. Support correspondence may be kept as needed to resolve a request, maintain records, prevent misuse, or meet legal obligations.

After an approved account-deletion request, ClothMatics removes the account and associated wardrobe, images, outfits, plans, history, challenge information, and other linked app data. Information may be retained only where the law permits or requires it.

7. Security and account access

We use reasonable safeguards to protect accounts and personal information, limit access, and reduce unauthorized use. Your wardrobe is available on the website only after you sign in. Limited authorized access may be used when needed for support, safety, privacy requests, or service administration.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong password, sign out on shared devices, and contact us if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a grievance. Many wardrobe and profile details can be updated in the mobile app.

To request permanent account deletion, open the Help and Contact deletion form, sign in to the account you want deleted, confirm the request, and submit it for review.

9. Age requirements

ClothMatics is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to consent to online services where they live. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information without proper permission should contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ClothMatics features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Important changes may also be communicated through the app, website, or registered email where appropriate.

11. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, information requests, account deletion, support, or grievances, use the ClothMatics Help and Contact page.

Open Help and Contact

Choose “Privacy or data request” and use the email connected to your account where possible. We may verify your identity before acting on an account request.
