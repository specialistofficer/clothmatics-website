Who we are

ClothMatics is an independently operated digital wardrobe and outfit-planning service offered under the ClothMatics name. It is not represented as a separate incorporated company.

The public website explains the product and provides help, policy, and photo-guidance resources. A signed-in user can also open the private web wardrobe connected to the same account used in the Android app.

Why ClothMatics exists

Many wardrobes contain useful clothes that are forgotten, repeated unevenly, or difficult to combine. ClothMatics is designed to make an owned wardrobe easier to understand and use before encouraging another purchase.

The service organizes garment information, brings complete looks and plans together, tracks wear activity, and can prepare styling or trip-packing suggestions grounded in eligible clothes already connected to the user's account.

How the product works

Mobile app

The Android app handles garment capture, garment-image changes, background preparation, and camera-based clothing features. It is the primary place to add clothes to a wardrobe.

Website

The website provides the public product information and a private, authenticated wardrobe companion. Signed-in users can review supported wardrobe details, saved outfits, Lookbook combinations, plans, wear insights, profile preferences, and Closet Quest progress.

AI assistance

Eligible signed-in features may use wardrobe metadata to prepare outfit or packing suggestions. Generated suggestions can be imperfect and remain subject to the user's judgment. The public website does not expose private wardrobe information to crawlers or discovery services.

Operating principles

  • Owned wardrobe first: help users find more combinations in clothes they already have.
  • Clear product boundaries: state which functions belong to mobile, web, or authenticated services.
  • Private by default: require sign-in before showing personal wardrobe information.
  • User control: provide routes for support, privacy questions, data requests, and account deletion.
  • Verifiable public information: keep canonical help, policy, sitemap, and machine-readable discovery resources on the official website.
