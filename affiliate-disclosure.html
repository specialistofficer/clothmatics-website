Affiliate links

ClothMatics may participate in affiliate marketing programs and work with affiliate networks such as Awin, Admitad, and Optimise Media.

When users discover products through ClothMatics, some product links may be affiliate links. If a user makes a purchase through an eligible affiliate link, ClothMatics may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate network at no additional cost to the user.

Product recommendations

Affiliate partnerships help support the development and operation of ClothMatics. Product recommendations are intended to help users discover relevant fashion products and are not influenced solely by affiliate compensation.

Product information

ClothMatics may display clothing, footwear, accessories, beauty, or other fashion products from third-party retailers. Where available, product information may include:

  • Product image
  • Product name
  • Brand
  • Price or discounted price
  • Retailer
  • Purchase link

The information available varies by product, retailer, and affiliate network; not every network provides every field. Check the retailer's website for current prices, availability, and purchase terms.
