Questions, meet answers.

Everything you need to know about your account, digital wardrobe, AI outfits, privacy, and the ClothMatics website.

GETTING STARTED

Account and access

Can I use the same account on the app and website?

Yes. Sign in with the same Firebase email/password or Google account. Your Firebase user ID connects the website to the wardrobe and saved outfits created in the mobile app.

Why can’t I sign in with Google?

Allow pop-ups, confirm you are using the same Google account as the app, and try a fresh browser tab. The website domain must also be authorized in Firebase Authentication. If the problem continues, send the displayed Firebase error code to support.

Does closing the browser sign me out?

No. ClothMatics uses browser-local Firebase session persistence. Use the Sign out button on a shared device.

WARDROBE

Your clothes and looks

Can I add clothes through the website?

Use the ClothMatics mobile app to add, extract, edit, or remove garments. The website is designed for browsing your closet, saved looks, plans, and wardrobe insights.

Why is my website wardrobe empty?

Make sure you signed in with the same account used in the mobile app. If the account is correct, refresh after confirming that the app finished saving the items.

Are favorites and saved outfits synchronized?

Yes. The website reads favorites, Lookbook items, and saved outfits from the same Firestore project used by the mobile app.

Can the website change laundry status?

No. The website can filter and use laundry status while styling, but changes must be made in the mobile app.

AI AND PRIVACY

Website processing

How does the web outfit generator work?

Choose an occasion and optional preference. ClothMatics sends relevant wardrobe metadata to a protected Cloudflare server function, which requests one outfit using only item IDs from your wardrobe.

Does the ordinary user website call Gemini?

No. Gemini and other generative-AI APIs are not called by ordinary user pages. AI-assisted outfit and garment features remain in the mobile app.

Does Smart Purchase upload the store photo?

No. The preview stays in your browser and the comparison uses local wardrobe metadata. It does not upload the photo or call an AI service.

PRIVACY & SUPPORT

Data and help

Who can see my wardrobe?

Firestore Security Rules restrict normal queries to the authenticated owner. A specifically authorized administrator may access operational data for support, security, and service administration.

How do I request my data or delete my account?

Open Help and Contact — Delete account, sign in to the exact account, confirm the permanent deletion, and submit the secure request. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where can I report a problem?

Use the Help and Contact form and include your platform, app version, what you attempted, and any visible error code. Never send your password, OTP, API keys, or payment information.

NEW WEB TOOLS

Lookbook, planner, purchase checks, festivals and sharing

What can I now change on the website?

You can create or delete your own Lookbook combinations and plan, remove, or mark outfits worn. Garment capture, editing, favorites, laundry status, profile answers, and Closet Quest submissions still belong in the mobile app.

Does Smart Purchase save the store photo?

No. The browser preview is transient, makes no AI request, and is not added to your wardrobe.

What is included when I share an outfit?

The share flow uses garment or outfit references, ClothMatics attribution, and an attributed redirect link. It does not add your email, date of birth, body details, or location.
