Delete Your ClothMatics Account & Personal Data

In compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play Store policies, ClothMatics provides transparent, permanent account and personal data erasure for all users.

Statutory Compliance: DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025 Fulfillment Window: Within 15 Business Days

1. How to Delete Your Account In-App (Recommended)

If you currently have the ClothMatics app installed on your Android device, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data immediately with zero waiting period:

1
Open ClothMatics & Go to Profile

Launch the ClothMatics app on your phone and tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.

2
Open Settings

Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) in the upper corner of your profile screen.

3
Select "Privacy & Data"

In the Settings menu, tap Privacy & Data to access your DPDP privacy controls and statutory options.

4
Tap "Delete my account"

Scroll down to the Danger Zone at the bottom of the screen and tap Delete my account.

5
Confirm Permanent Deletion

Read the permanent erasure summary and tap Permanently Delete My Account. Your sign-in credentials, wardrobe items, photos, and history are immediately purged.

Tip: Before deleting your account, you can tap "Export personal data" in the Privacy & Data screen to download a copy of all your wardrobe records in machine-readable JSON format.

2. Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled or Inaccessible)

If you have uninstalled the ClothMatics app, no longer have access to your mobile device, or prefer to request deletion via the web, you have two options under Section 12(3) of the DPDP Act:

Option A: Direct Email Request (Web Fallback)

Send an email from the email address registered with your ClothMatics account to our privacy support desk:

  • To: clothmatics@gmail.com
  • Subject: ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST: <your-registered-email@example.com>
  • Body text: "I request the permanent deletion of my ClothMatics account and all associated personal data. Registered Email: [your email]."

Response Timeline: Your request will be reviewed and permanently processed by our administration team within 30 days.

Option B: Authenticated Web Deletion Form

You can also sign in and submit a deletion request directly through our web portal: Help & Contact — Web Deletion Request.

3. Scope of Data: What is Deleted vs Retained

When an account deletion request is approved and processed by our administrator, ClothMatics permanently erases all user data:

Data Category Deletion Status Details & Storage Scope
Authentication & Profile Permanently Deleted Google sign-in ID, registered email address, display name, avatar, and style preferences (measurements, styling tags) are completely wiped.
Garment Photos & Cutouts Permanently Deleted All original user-uploaded clothing photos and transparent background-removed cutouts stored in encrypted cloud storage are permanently purged.
Digital Wardrobe Items Permanently Deleted All clothing records, categories, brands, seasons, colors, and notes in the database are erased.
Outfits, Plans & History Permanently Deleted Saved outfit combinations, lookbooks, packing lists, wear frequencies, and calendar wear records are permanently destroyed.
Consents & Push Tokens Permanently Deleted FCM notification tokens, active marketing preferences, and in-app consent toggles are purged immediately.
Security & Processing Logs Temporary Retention Pseudonymised server and security access logs (containing no photos, garments, or personal styling text) are retained for 1 month and automatically purged thereafter.

4. Timelines & Administrative Processing

Submission: Once you submit an account deletion request (in-app or via our web form/email), your request is queued for administrative review.

Processing Window: Our administration team reviews and permanently deletes your authentication account, database documents, and uploaded images within 30 days.
