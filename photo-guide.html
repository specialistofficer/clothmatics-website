What are you doing?

Add to Closet and Style Check need slightly different photos.

01
Add to ClosetOne garment

Show one complete garment

  • Lay it flat or place it on a hanger.
  • Keep sleeves, collar, hem, and every edge visible.
  • Use a background that contrasts with the garment.
Best choiceA single item with space around every edge.
02
Style CheckComplete outfit

Show the full styled look

  • Include every important layer and your footwear.
  • Use a neutral pose with one person in the frame.
  • Avoid mirrors, filters, crossed arms, and covered garments.
Best choiceA full, front-facing outfit with balanced lighting.
